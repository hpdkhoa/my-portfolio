**Hosting the HTML:** the pages are self contained (only CDN fonts + MathJax are external). To
publish them, enable **GitHub Pages** on this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from branch), and
they'll be live at `https://hpdkhoa.github.io/engineering-portfolio/writeups/…`. Then link those
URLs from your LinkedIn Featured section. Opening the `.html` files locally in a browser also works.
